About this dedication

Shoxista Sassigista is the personal, symbolic name used on this page for a dedication to Sirius, the brightest star in Earth's night sky. The name is a tribute; it does not replace the star's scientific name in astronomical catalogues.

Sirius is located in the constellation Canis Major (the Great Dog) and is famous for its brilliant blue-white appearance.
